# Zero-Cost AI Compliance Guide – Principles Overview

A strategic, cost-free approach to AI governance that focuses on **aligning your AI systems with universal AI principles**.

---

## Purpose
This guide helps organizations **understand and apply AI principles** to ensure responsible, ethical, and auditable AI practices.  
It does **not provide operational step-by-step instructions**, but highlights the principles every AI project should consider.

---

## AI Principles Overview

1. **Accountability** – Assign responsibility for AI systems, ensuring owners are identifiable and decisions can be traced.  

2. **Fairness_and_Non-Discrimination** – Avoid bias and ensure equitable treatment for all individuals and groups.  

3. **Governance** – Implement structured oversight frameworks to manage AI risks and compliance.  

4. **Human_Oversight** – Maintain human supervision over critical AI decisions, preserving human agency.  

5. **Privacy_and_Data_Protection** – Protect personal data and comply with privacy regulations.  

6. **Transparency_and_Explicability** – Ensure AI decisions are understandable and explainable to stakeholders.  

7. **Traceability** – Maintain documentation and logs to allow audits and retrospective review.  

8. **Environment_and_Technological_Sustainability** – Minimize environmental impact and promote sustainable AI practices.  

9. **LLM_Security** – Secure AI systems, particularly large language models, against misuse and vulnerabilities.  

10. **Open_Source_and_Collaborative_Governance** – Promote transparency, collaboration, and responsible use in open-source AI projects.

---

## Strategic Recommendations

- **Map principles to your AI systems**: Understand which principles are relevant for each model or project.  
- **Assess principle coverage**: Identify gaps or risks in each principle area.  
- **Use free tools strategically**: Documentation and monitoring can be done using spreadsheets, wikis, or basic dashboards.  
- **Leverage your platform**: Automate principle mapping, tracking, and reporting with the AI Governance platform to save time and ensure consistency.  

---

## Notes

- This guide is **principle-focused** and suitable for early adoption or awareness purposes.  
- Full compliance and audit readiness should leverage **structured platforms and formal policies**.  
- Demonstrates your organization’s commitment to **responsible and auditable AI**, even at zero cost.
