# AI-Governance-Free

**Open-source AI governance project** focused on principles, not laws.  
We believe laws evolve and differ across regions, but principles are universal.  
This project provides compliance guides, checklists, and templates mapped to global principles of responsible AI, grounded in international frameworks.

---

## 📌 Principles Considered

The following principles are the foundation of this repository.  
For each principle, we cite the main international frameworks and regulations that provide support.

### 1. Accountability
- **AI Act (EU)** – assigns responsibility to providers and deployers.  
- **ISO/IEC 42001** – management system with clear roles and responsibilities.  
- **NIST AI RMF** – “Govern” function emphasizes accountability.  
- **UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of AI (2021)** – accountability and redress as global ethical requirements.  

### 2. Fairness_and_Non-Discrimination
- **AI Act** – prohibits discriminatory AI uses.  
- **ISO/IEC 42001** – requires bias identification and mitigation.  
- **NIST AI RMF** – fairness metrics and monitoring.  
- **UNESCO AI Ethics** – fairness as a universal principle.  

### 3. Governance
- **AI Act** – compliance and regulatory oversight for high-risk systems.  
- **ISO/IEC 42001** – governance framework for AI lifecycle management.  
- **NIST AI RMF** – governance as the foundation.  
- **UNESCO AI Ethics** – calls for robust AI governance.  

### 4. Human_Oversight
- **AI Act** – mandatory human oversight for high-risk AI.  
- **ISO/IEC 42001** – roles for human monitoring and intervention.  
- **NIST AI RMF** – human in the loop for critical functions.  
- **UNESCO AI Ethics** – human agency and decision-making must be preserved.  

### 5. Privacy_and_Data_Protection
- **AI Act** – alignment with GDPR for lawful data use.  
- **ISO/IEC 42001** – data protection and governance processes.  
- **NIST AI RMF** – privacy risk management.  
- **UNESCO AI Ethics** – privacy as a global human right.  

### 6. Transparency_and_Explicability
- **AI Act** – transparency obligations and documentation.  
- **ISO/IEC 42001** – explainability and lifecycle documentation.  
- **NIST AI RMF** – interpretability as a trustworthiness factor.  
- **UNESCO AI Ethics** – transparency and explainability required.  

### 7. traceability
- **AI Act** – logging obligations for high-risk systems.  
- **ISO/IEC 42001** – record-keeping throughout lifecycle.  
- **NIST AI RMF** – promotes traceability as part of accountability.  
- **UNESCO AI Ethics** – requires traceability and auditability.  

### 8. Environment_and_Technological_Sustainability
- **AI Act** – indirectly through safety and robustness (limited).  
- **ISO/IEC 42001** – mentions sustainability considerations.  
- **NIST AI RMF** – does not directly cover.  
- **UNESCO AI Ethics** – explicitly requires environmental sustainability.  

### 9. LLM_Security
- **AI Act** – general robustness and resilience obligations, no LLM-specific provisions.  
- **ISO/IEC 42001** – broad information security controls, not LLM-specific.  
- **NIST AI RMF** – security as part of risk management, but not LLM-focused.  
- **UNESCO AI Ethics** – not directly addressed.  

### 10. Open_Source_and_Collaborative_Governance
- **AI Act** – limited application to open-source AI models.  
- **ISO/IEC 42001** – no direct mention of open-source governance.  
- **NIST AI RMF** – encourages collaborative governance.  
- **UNESCO AI Ethics** – encourages international collaboration.

## 🎯 Objective
To deliver **free, principle-based governance resources** (guides, checklists, templates) that organizations can adopt immediately to align with the highest international standards.

## 👥 Target Audience
- IT and AI professionals  
- Technology recruiters  
- Clients seeking responsible and auditable AI  

## 📂 Project Structure
- `01-Docs-Requisitos/`: Principle-based regulatory mappings  
- `02-Guides/`: Practical implementation guides  
- `03-Checklists/`: Audit checklists and case studies  
- `04-Templates/`: Reusable policy and risk templates  
- `05-Extras/`: Comparative and supplementary materials
