# AI Governance Principles – Multilevel Foundations

This document defines the **10 universal principles** guiding the AI-Governance-Free project.  
Principles are supported by **multilateral frameworks (UNESCO, OCDE, UN), regional standards (EU AI Act, GDPR, ISO 42001, NIST AI RMF)**, and **national regulations** (Brazil, USA, Canada, Japan, China, Australia, etc.).  
The goal is **principles-first AI governance**, ensuring ethical and responsible AI globally.

---

## 1. Accountability
AI systems must have clear lines of responsibility for design, deployment, and oversight.

**Multilateral References:**  
- UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of AI (2021)  
- OECD AI Principles (2019)  
- UN Human Rights Council AI Reports (2021–2023)  

**Regional References:**  
- AI Act (EU, 2024)  
- ISO/IEC 42001 – AI Management System  
- NIST AI RMF (US, 2023)  

**National References:**  
- 🇧🇷 LGPD (Brazil, 2018)  
- 🇺🇸 AI Executive Order (2023), CCPA (California, 2018)  
- 🇨🇦 AIDA (draft, 2023)  
- 🇯🇵 AI Governance Guidelines (2022)  
- 🇨🇳 Provisions on Generative AI Services (2023)  
- 🇦🇺 AI Ethics Principles (2019)
