# AI Risk Assessment Template – Principles-Based

## Objective
Identify, assess, and mitigate risks associated with AI systems, **aligned with the 10 universal AI principles**.

---

## Template Structure by Principle

1. **Model Audited**  
   - Name, type, purpose, and scope of the AI system.  
   - Related Principles: **Accountability, Governance**

2. **Principle Coverage Assessment**  
   - List relevant principles for this model (from `00-Principles.md`).  
   - Example: Accountability, Fairness, Privacy.  

3. **Potential Risks per Principle**  
   - Describe risks in relation to each principle:  
     - **Accountability:** unclear ownership or oversight gaps  
     - **Fairness:** biased outputs or unequal treatment  
     - **Human Oversight:** critical decisions without human supervision  
     - **Privacy:** data exposure or regulatory non-compliance  
     - **Transparency:** unexplainable or opaque decisions  
     - **Traceability:** missing logs or audit trails  
     - **Environment:** high energy consumption or unsustainable computing  
     - **LLM Security:** vulnerabilities, adversarial attacks  
     - **Open-Source Governance:** unverified dependencies or licenses  

4. **Probability of Occurrence / Severity**  
   - Evaluate likelihood and impact of each risk.  
   - Related Principles: **Governance, Accountability**  

5. **Existing Controls**  
   - Current measures addressing each principle-related risk.  
   - Related Principles: **All principles depending on mitigation measures**

6. **Mitigation Actions / Recommendations**  
   - High-level actions to reduce principle-related risks (no operational details).  
   - Related Principles: **All principles**

7. **Responsible Party**  
   - Assign accountability for risk management and mitigation.  
   - Related Principles: **Accountability, Governance, Human Oversight**

8. **Status / Monitoring**  
   - Track whether risks are mitigated, ongoing, or need attention.  
   - Related Principles: **Traceability, Governance, Transparency**

---

## Notes
- This template is **principle-first**, linking every risk to one or more universal AI principles.  
- It is suitable for **initial and ongoing risk assessments** of AI systems.  
- Designed to complement **AI Policy Templates, Audit Checklists, and Principles Mapping**, forming a complete AI governance toolkit.
