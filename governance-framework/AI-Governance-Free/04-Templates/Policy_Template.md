# AI Policy Template – Principles-Based

## Objective
Establish internal rules and governance for responsible, ethical, and auditable AI use, **fully aligned with the 10 universal AI principles**.

---

## Suggested Structure by Principle

1. **Accountability**  
   - Define ownership and responsibility for all AI systems.  
   - Ensure clear reporting lines for design, deployment, and monitoring.  

2. **Fairness_and_Non-Discrimination**  
   - Establish policies to identify and mitigate bias.  
   - Ensure AI decisions promote equitable treatment for all individuals and groups.  

3. **Governance**  
   - Implement structured oversight and management processes for AI projects.  
   - Define roles for compliance, risk assessment, and decision-making.  

4. **Human_Oversight**  
   - Define procedures for human supervision over AI decisions.  
   - Establish intervention mechanisms for critical or high-risk actions.  

5. **Privacy_and_Data_Protection**  
   - Ensure personal data is processed according to relevant privacy laws (LGPD, GDPR, etc.).  
   - Define guidelines for data collection, storage, and usage.  

6. **Transparency_and_Explicability**  
   - Require documentation of AI models, data sources, and decision logic.  
   - Make model decisions understandable to relevant stakeholders.  

7. **Traceability**  
   - Maintain logs and records for model inputs, outputs, and decisions.  
   - Support audit and retrospective analysis.  

8. **Environment_and_Technological_Sustainability**  
   - Define measures to minimize environmental impact.  
   - Encourage efficient computing and resource usage.  

9. **LLM_Security**  
   - Establish policies for secure use of AI systems, especially large language models.  
   - Address risks of misuse, adversarial attacks, and unauthorized access.  

10. **Open_Source_and_Collaborative_Governance**  
    - Define guidelines for responsible use of open-source AI.  
    - Promote collaboration, transparency, and shared governance practices.  

---

## Periodic Review and Updates
- Schedule regular reviews to align the policy with evolving AI principles, technology, and regulations.  
- Update roles, responsibilities, and safeguards as needed.

---

## Notes
- This template is **principle-first**, providing a **high-level governance framework** for internal AI policies.  
- Can be adapted to specific organizational contexts, risk profiles, and regulatory environments.  
- Designed to complement **principles mapping and audit checklists** for a complete governance program.
