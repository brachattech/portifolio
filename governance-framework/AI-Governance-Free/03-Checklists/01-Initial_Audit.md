# Initial AI Audit Checklist – Principles-Based

A structured, principle-focused checklist for conducting an initial AI audit.  
Designed for **early-stage compliance and governance assessment**.

---

## Model Audited
- Example: Simple recommendation or classification bot  

---

## Verification Items by AI Principle

1. **Accountability**  
   - Identify owners/responsible parties for the model  
   - Document roles for design, deployment, and oversight  

2. **Fairness_and_Non-Discrimination**  
   - Assess model outputs for bias and equitable treatment  
   - Review dataset diversity and representativeness  

3. **Governance**  
   - Confirm existence of oversight and decision-making structures  
   - Ensure policies for risk management and compliance are defined  

4. **Human_Oversight**  
   - Verify humans can intervene in critical decisions  
   - Check human-in-the-loop mechanisms where necessary  

5. **Privacy_and_Data_Protection**  
   - Ensure data sources comply with privacy regulations (GDPR, LGPD, etc.)  
   - Document consent and anonymization processes  

6. **Transparency_and_Explicability**  
   - Review model explanations and documentation  
   - Ensure stakeholders can understand decision logic  

7. **Traceability**  
   - Verify logs of inputs, outputs, and decisions exist  
   - Ensure audit trails can reconstruct model behavior  

8. **Environment_and_Technological_Sustainability**  
   - Assess energy consumption and compute efficiency  
   - Evaluate lifecycle sustainability considerations  

9. **LLM_Security**  
   - Check security measures for model access and manipulation  
   - Evaluate robustness against adversarial attacks  

10. **Open_Source_and_Collaborative_Governance**  
    - Ensure open-source components follow licensing and contribution policies  
    - Promote collaborative oversight and responsible usage  

---

## Notes
- This checklist is **principle-first**, aligned with international frameworks (AI Act, ISO 42001, NIST AI RMF) and national regulations.  
- Suitable for **initial audits** and early adoption phases.  
- Intended to **identify gaps** before implementing full compliance systems or platforms.
